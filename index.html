Optimal Wordle Solver

PythonAlgorithmic Design

Developed an information-theoretic Wordle solver that selects each guess by maximizing expected information gain (entropy) across the remaining word space, narrowing down the solution in a minimal number of guesses. Validated against 100k simulations with a 99.905% accuracy.
